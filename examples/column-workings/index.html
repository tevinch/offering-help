Column workings

Write the steps yourself, one square at a time.

Click a square and type a digit. Typing moves right; arrow keys move between squares. Backspace clears a digit. The starting numbers stay in place.

For a carry or borrowed number, select its square, choose Small number and type up to two digits, such as 10. Escape returns to the main square. Cross out keeps the original digit visible. Underline row adds or removes a line below that row.

Whole numbers up to 8 digits. Subtraction must have a nonnegative answer; division by zero is unavailable. This page does not calculate or check your work. Print to keep it: a new page or reload clears the current work.
